# Massachusetts public housing grievance

Help a current Massachusetts public housing tenant start a grievance or ask for a hearing after a meeting with staff did not solve the problem. The problem must affect the tenant's own household. The interview makes a letter and instructions to print, sign, and send to the housing authority.

It does not handle housing applications, voucher disputes, appeals of hearing decisions, or eviction court answers. Some eviction disputes may not qualify for a grievance hearing; the interview directs users to their notice, lease, housing authority rules, and legal help.

Entry point: `docassemble.MAGrievanceComplaint:data/questions/lt3grievancecomplaint.yml`.

For repairs, [UpToCode](https://uptocode.org/) is another path; a grievance is not required first. [MADE](https://www.gbls.org/MADE) helps with eviction court papers.

The letters are adaptations guided by:

- [MLRI sample letters and forms](https://www.masslegalhelp.org/housing-apartments-shelter/public-subsidized-housing/sample-letters-forms-and-worksheets)
- [Filing a grievance](https://www.masslegalhelp.org/housing-apartments-shelter/public-subsidized-housing/filing-grievance)
- [Which disputes qualify](https://www.masslegalhelp.org/housing-apartments-shelter/public-subsidized-housing/types-grievances)
- [Reviewing a grievance decision](https://www.masslegalhelp.org/housing-apartments-shelter/public-subsidized-housing/getting-grievance-decision)
- [760 CMR 6.08](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/760-CMR-6-08) and [24 CFR 966.54](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/24/966.54)
