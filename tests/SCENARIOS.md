# Release scenarios

1. **State-funded public housing, first repair grievance.** Tenant asks the authority to fix a reported leak. Expect an initial grievance letter, uncut narrative, correct tenant and recipient, and delivery instructions. Generated PDF must not claim a prior informal meeting. Edit the requested remedy and assert that the regenerated PDF includes the new text and removes the old text. Switch to the appeal off-ramp, then correct the stage without losing the tenant or edited remedy.
2. **Federal public housing, rent dispute after an informal meeting.** Tenant disputes an unpaid-rent allegation and faces termination. Expect a renewed hearing request and eviction warning, with no categorical denial based on an allegation. PDF must describe the unresolved meeting, preserve the requested correction, and not be the initial-grievance variant.
3. **State tenant has a hearing decision.** Expect the appeal off-ramp, relevant deadline warning and sample-letter links, with no initial-grievance document offered.

These synthetic examples test preparation and output, not a ruling that any grievance is legally available. They do not cover every housing authority's policy. Long narratives and mobile layout should also be checked before launch.
